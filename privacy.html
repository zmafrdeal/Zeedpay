Privacy Policy

How Zeedpay collects, uses, shares, protects, and retains information.

Effective: 05 September 2026Zeedpay Technologies Ltd.
Important: This policy is a product-facing draft based on the current Zeedpay application. Zeedpay should confirm the applicable controller, processor, cross-border, retention, and user-rights requirements with qualified privacy counsel before publication.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeedpay Technologies Ltd. (“Zeedpay”, “we”, “us” or “our”) processes personal information when you create an account, use a Zeedpay merchant wallet, visit a Zeedpay-hosted payment page, contact support, or otherwise interact with the service.

For the account and merchant services described here, Zeedpay generally acts as the organisation responsible for deciding why and how information is processed. Where Zeedpay processes information for a merchant’s customers on the merchant’s instructions, the merchant may have additional responsibilities and privacy notices.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: full name, business name, email address, phone number, country, merchant ID, referral code, password-related records, transaction PIN status, and security settings.
  • Financial and transaction information: wallet identifiers, wallet balances, currency, payment references, amounts, fees, payment method, provider reference, transaction status, ledger entries, bank or mobile-money details supplied for a transaction, and payment-link or money-pool activity.
  • Verification information: documents, information, or checks needed for identity, business, fraud, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, tax, or regulatory requirements.
  • Customer and donor information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, and contribution information that you or a payer submit through a payment link or money pool.
  • Support and communication information: feedback, messages, notification preferences, support requests, and records of our communications.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, browser or device information, session and access records, authentication events, error data, and security-event metadata.

Please do not place unnecessary sensitive personal information in a public payment-link description, money-pool description, feedback message, or payment reference.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and administer accounts, authenticate users, provide wallets and payment features, send payment instructions, reconcile ledger entries, display transaction history, process referrals and commissions, manage investment-wallet records, provide support, send service and security notifications, prevent fraud and abuse, investigate disputes, maintain and improve the service, and meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the applicable law and the context, we process information because it is needed to perform a contract with you, to take steps at your request before entering a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, to protect users and the service, or for our legitimate operational interests. Where consent is required, we will request it and you may withdraw it as permitted by law.

5. Payments and sharing

To provide payment services, we may share relevant information with mobile-money operators, card networks, banks, payment processors, identity and verification providers, fraud-prevention services, cloud and infrastructure providers, communications providers, auditors, professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, or other service providers acting on our instructions.

We do not sell personal information as a source of advertising revenue. We share only information reasonably needed for the purpose, subject to appropriate contractual, security, or legal safeguards where required.

6. Public payment pages

Payment links and money pools are designed to be shareable. Information you choose to publish on a public page—including a title, description, amount, image, business information, or donor-facing instructions—may be visible to anyone who receives the link. You are responsible for providing any notices required for information you collect from your customers or donors.

7. Cookies and local storage

The web application may use browser storage to remember session state, preferences, cached wallet information, notification status, and install or display choices. We may use essential cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security, load balancing, and core operation. Optional analytics or marketing technologies should be disclosed and controlled through the applicable consent mechanism before being enabled.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information, which may include encrypted connections, access controls, session expiry, transaction PINs, two-factor authentication, audit logs, monitoring, database controls, and provider security processes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so keep account credentials private and notify us promptly about suspected unauthorised access.

9. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet tax or regulatory obligations, enforce agreements, and establish or defend legal claims. Retention periods vary by record type and applicable law. When information is no longer needed, we will delete, anonymise, or securely isolate it where reasonably practicable.

10. International processing

Zeedpay and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live or operate. Where required, we will use an appropriate legal mechanism or safeguard for the transfer and provide information about it through an applicable notice or request process.

11. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your information; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a competent data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity, protect other people’s rights, preserve records required by law, or decline a request that would compromise security or another lawful interest.

You can update certain profile details in the app. To make a privacy request, use the current support contact shown in About and describe the account, information, and action you are requesting.

12. Children

Zeedpay is intended for businesses and adults who can enter a binding agreement. We do not knowingly design the service for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can assess and take appropriate action.

13. Third-party links and services

Zeedpay may link to payment providers, banks, social sharing tools, or other third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. Review those notices before providing information directly to them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version and revise the effective date. If a change is material, we will use a reasonable in-app or account communication where appropriate.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the current Zeedpay support contacts shown in the app’s About section. The fields below are populated from the platform’s saved public settings.
